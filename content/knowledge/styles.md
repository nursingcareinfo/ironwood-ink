# Tattoo Styles at Ironwood Ink

The studio works in eight signature styles. Every piece is custom-drawn.

## Japanese / Irezumi
Traditional Japanese tattooing with roots in te-bori (hand-poking) tradition.
Characterized by bold outlines, large background areas (waves, wind bars, clouds),
and motifs drawn from folklore: koi, dragons, tigers, chrysanthemums, peonies,
hannya masks. Sleeves and back pieces are designed as one continuous composition
that flows with the body. Practiced by Mara Voss, who apprenticed under a te-bori
master in Osaka.

## American Traditional
The old-school canon: roses, daggers, swallows, panthers, hearts, anchors. Heavy
black outlines, limited bold palette (red, yellow, green, blue), no grey wash
fades. Designed to read from across the room and hold for decades. Practiced by
Jon Bell.

## Blackwork
Solid black saturation and negative space. Ranges from delicate stipple shading
to fully packed panels. High contrast, graphic, no color. Favored for abstract
composition and cover-up work. Practiced by Mara Voss.

## Fine Line
Single-needle precision work: botanical florals, script, jewelry-fine details.
Low pain, high elegance, ages softly. Practiced by Sofia Reyes.

## Neo-Traditional
Traditional composition with contemporary depth: painterly shading, expressive
color palettes, illustrative textures. Motifs often rework classic subjects
(animals, daggers, flowers) with modern symbolism. Practiced by Jon Bell.

## Realism / Portrait
Photographic portraiture and photo-real subjects. Requires hours of reference
study, precise value control, and a steady hand. Black-and-grey or color.
Practiced by Sofia Reyes.

## Dotwork
Sacred geometry, mandalas, stippled texture built from thousands of individual
dots. Meditative, symmetrical, often symmetrical around the spine or sternum.
Practiced by Jon Bell.

## Tribal / Polynesian
Bold angular patterns drawn from Polynesian heritage traditions. Designed with
respect for cultural origin and fitted to individual anatomy. Flow and direction
matter as much as pattern.
