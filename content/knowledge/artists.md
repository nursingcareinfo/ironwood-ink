# Artists at Ironwood Ink

Three resident artists, three distinct voices. All work is custom — the studio
does not tattoo stencil-catalog flash.

## Mara Voss — Founder
Twenty years behind the machine, twelve of them apprenticed under a te-bori
master in Osaka. Specialist in Japanese / irezumi and blackwork. Designs every
background element by hand — wind bars, waves, clouds — so no two pieces match.
Also performs te-bori (hand-poked) sessions on request. Leads the studio's
apprenticeship program.

## Jon Bell
Trained through a two-year apprenticeship on the American Traditional curriculum.
Specialist in American traditional and neo-traditional. Known for heavy black
outlines and full-spectrum color packing. His flash wall is a curated collection
of classic 1940s designs. Also practices dotwork / sacred geometry.

## Sofia Reyes
Former illustrator who transitioned to tattooing. Specialist in fine line
botanical work and realism / portrait. Precise single-needle control, patient
multi-session portrait work from original photographs. Longest waiting list in
the studio — bookings open quarterly.

## Studio principles
- Custom design only: every piece starts as a conversation.
- Vegan inks and single-use needles, always.
- Free touch-ups within six months; lifetime touch-ups on large pieces.
- Aftercare kit + printed guide with every piece; free 2–4 week check-up.
